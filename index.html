Child lock

How to turn on screen pinning
  1. Open Android Settings → Security (or Security & privacy) → More security settings → App pinning. Turn it on.
  2. Turn on Ask for PIN before unpinning so a child can't unpin.
  3. Come back here and tick Lock to Pip. Android asks once to confirm.
  4. To unlock, hold Parent, answer the questions, and untick Lock to Pip.
